HOW TO PRIVACY POLICY

LMS GamerMix — Privacy Policy

Effective 27 August 2026

LMS GamerMix does not collect your data. There is no account to create, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not run a server, so there is nowhere for your information to be sent.

Who we are

LMS GamerMix is made by Lookout Mountain Systems, LLC. In this policy, “we” means that company. You can reach us at support@lookoutmtsystems.com, or by mail at 2108 N St. #12526, Sacramento, CA 95816.

What we collect

Nothing. We collect no category of personal information: no name, email address, postal address, phone number, location, contacts, device identifier, advertising identifier and no usage analytics. There is no account to create and no form to fill in.

Stored on your device

Everything the app saves stays in its own storage on your iPhone:

  • Sounds and images you load onto pads
  • Saved programs, meaning your pad layouts
  • Effect settings and levels
  • The address of the PC you last connected to
  • A saved pairing so the app can recognise the same PC next time

We cannot see any of it. None of it is sent to us, and deleting the app removes all of it from your phone. Because we hold nothing, there is no retention period on our side and nothing for us to delete.

Your microphone

Audio is captured only while you are connected and streaming. It goes directly to the PC you are using, over your own network. The audio is encrypted. It does not pass through us, it is not recorded by the app, and it is not stored on your phone.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple, not by us. We never see your name, your payment details or your Apple Account. Apple tells the app only whether a subscription is currently active.

Apple also gives us aggregated sales reports that count purchases by country. Those reports do not identify anyone. What Apple itself collects is governed by Apple’s privacy policy, not this one.

Analytics and third parties

The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software of any kind, and does not use advertising identifiers. No third party collects information about you through this app, and nothing in it follows you across other apps or websites.

We do not sell or share personal information with anyone, including data brokers, and we never have, because we do not have any to sell.

Do Not Track

Some devices and browsers can send a Do Not Track signal. We do not need to act on one: the app performs no tracking of any kind and follows you nowhere, so there is nothing for such a signal to switch off.

Crash reports

If you have turned on Share With App Developers in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security, Analytics & Improvements, Apple may send us crash reports. Those come from Apple and are not linked to you. You can turn that setting off at any time.

Your rights

Laws in California, the European Union, the United Kingdom and elsewhere give you the right to see, correct, delete or export the personal information a company holds about you. We hold none, so there is nothing for us to hand over or erase. The information the app saves is on your phone, under your control, and deleting the app deletes it.

You are welcome to write to us at support@lookoutmtsystems.com or at 2108 N St. #12526, Sacramento, CA 95816 to ask anything about this. If you are in the EU or UK, you also have the right to complain to your national data protection authority.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the copy in the app and the copy on the web are updated together and the effective date above moves. Material changes are also described in the release notes of the app update that introduces them. If we ever begin collecting anything, this policy will say so before we do.
