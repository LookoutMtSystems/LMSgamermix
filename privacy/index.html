LMS GamerMix — Privacy Policy

Effective 24 August 2026

LMS GamerMix does not collect your data. There is no account to create, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not run a server, so there is nowhere for your information to be sent.

Stored on your device

Everything the app saves stays in its own storage on your iPhone and is deleted when you delete the app:

  • Sounds and images you load onto pads
  • Saved programs, meaning your pad layouts
  • Effect settings and levels
  • The address of the PC you last connected to
  • A fingerprint of your PC’s certificate, so the app can recognise the same PC next time

None of it is transmitted to us or to anyone else.

Your microphone

Audio is captured only while you are connected and streaming. It goes directly to the PC whose address you entered, over your own network, encrypted. It does not pass through us, it is not recorded by the app, and it is not stored on your phone.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We never see your name, your payment details or your Apple Account. Apple tells the app only whether a subscription is currently active.

Analytics and third parties

The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software of any kind, and does not use advertising identifiers. We do not sell, rent or share information with anyone, including data brokers, because we do not have any to share.

Crash reports

If you have turned on Share With App Developers in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security, Analytics & Improvements, Apple may send us crash reports. Those come from Apple and are not linked to you.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the copy in the app and the copy on the web are updated together and the date above moves. If we ever begin collecting anything, this page will say so before we do.
