LMS GamerMix — How To

Windows app v0.2.0

LMS GamerMix turns your iPhone into a microphone and soundboard for your PC. You need this app and the Windows app. They talk over your Wi-Fi. Nothing goes through the internet.

Get the PC app

Windows 11, 64-bit. The installer is LMSGamerMix-0.2.0-Setup.exe and it includes the app plus the virtual microphone driver.

Connect

  1. Put the phone and the PC on the same Wi-Fi. Guest networks and some routers’ “client isolation” or “AP isolation” settings will block the two from talking.
  2. Open LMS GamerMix on the phone. Allow the microphone and Local Network when iOS asks.
  3. Tap START. The yellow address is this phone. Leave it running.
  4. On the PC, open LMS GamerMix, type that address, and connect.
  5. In your game, voice chat, or Windows sound settings, pick LMS GamerMix as the microphone.

Default control port is 58710. Change it only if the PC is set to something else — gear icon on the phone.

Soundboard

Tap a pad to play. Hold a pad to load a sound or a GIF. Sounds mix into what the PC hears.

Free mode keeps the full mic and pad 1. The other pads, FX, levels and saved programs are Pro.

While you play

The mic stays on with the screen locked. It stops if you tap STOP, or if another app takes over audio — a call, a video, music.

Won’t connect

Same Wi-Fi, START showing WAITING FOR PC, and Local Network on for this app: Settings > Privacy & Security > Local Network. If the yellow address looks wrong, tap STOP then START again.
